Brief description

Software engineering is trending toward heightened autonomy and intelligence. Agentic AI and autonomous agents go beyond standard AI: they pursue goals, adapt to their environment, and communicate.

A primary aim of this winter school is to give participants a foundational grasp of Agentic AI through a software engineering lens. The curriculum connects theoretical frameworks with real-world implementation, exploring both directions of the relationship — Agentic AI for software engineering, and the engineering requirements of AI agents themselves.

Through lectures, practical labs and collaborative brainstorming, attendees engage with large language models and generative tools. The school covers the design and coordination of autonomous agents, decision-making frameworks, and tool-based agent construction.

It also addresses scalability, reliability, sustainability and the carbon footprint of AI-intensive systems.

Topics covered

Five themed modules across ten days. The day-wise schedule will be announced.

  1. 01

    Introduction to Agentic AI, and how AI agents work

    • Fundamentals of AI agents
    • Traditional AI versus Agentic AI
    • What “agency” means in AI
    • Architecture of AI agents
    • The agent loop — plan, act, observe, reflect

  2. 02

    Agentic AI in software engineering

    • Coding agents
    • Testing agents
    • Debugging agents
    • DevOps, release and support agents
    • Documentation and onboarding agents

  3. 03

    Software engineering for Agentic AI

    • Requirements for agentic systems
    • Agent architecture and orchestration
    • Observability and traceability
    • Testing and evaluation
    • Safety, permissions, rollback, guardrails
    • Cost, latency, reliability, sustainability
    • Governance and human oversight

  4. 04

    Hands-on mini project

    • Apply agentic concepts practically
    • Build an agentic framework with real SE concerns
    • Experience AI-assisted development

  5. 05

    Ethics, limitations and the future

    • Risks and limitations
    • The future of AI in software engineering
