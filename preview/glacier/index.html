Brief description

Software engineering is trending toward heightened autonomy and intelligence. Agentic AI and autonomous agents go beyond standard AI by integrating goal-oriented operation, environmental adaptability and sophisticated communication.

A primary aim of this winter school is to give participants a foundational grasp of Agentic AI through a software engineering lens. The curriculum connects theoretical frameworks with real-world implementation, exploring both directions of the relationship — Agentic AI for software engineering, and the engineering requirements of AI agents themselves.

Through lectures, practical labs and collaborative brainstorming, attendees engage with large language models and generative tools: the design and coordination of autonomous agents, decision-making frameworks, and tool-based agent construction. Sessions also address scalability, reliability, sustainability and the carbon footprint of AI-intensive systems.

The school aims to leave behind a research community, not just a cohort.

  1. i

    Practical capability

    Participants leave able to build and deploy AI agents that improve software quality and developer productivity.

  2. ii

    Research momentum

    Multi-agent cooperation, symbolic AI integration and trustworthy AI get pushed forward.

  3. iii

    A community

    A standing research community around AI-infused software engineering processes.

Four

Topics covered

Five themed modules across ten days. The day-wise schedule will be announced.

  1. 01

    Introduction to Agentic AI, and how AI agents work

    • Fundamentals of AI agents
    • Traditional AI versus Agentic AI
    • What “agency” means in AI
    • Architecture of AI agents
    • The agent loop — plan, act, observe, reflect

  2. 02

    Agentic AI in software engineering

    • Coding agents
    • Testing agents
    • Debugging agents
    • DevOps, release and support agents
    • Documentation and onboarding agents

  3. 03

    Software engineering for Agentic AI

    • Requirements for agentic systems
    • Agent architecture and orchestration
    • Observability and traceability
    • Testing and evaluation
    • Safety, permissions, rollback, guardrails
    • Cost, latency, reliability, sustainability
    • Governance and human oversight

  4. 04

    Hands-on mini project

    • Apply agentic concepts practically
    • Build an agentic framework with real SE concerns
    • Experience AI-assisted development

  5. 05

    Ethics, limitations and the future

    • Risks and limitations
    • The future of AI in software engineering

How the days are built

  • Research talks

    The rationale and methods behind integrating AI into SE practice.

  • Tutorial sessions

    Practical application, industry practice and honest limitations.

  • Hands-on labs

    Experiment with building tools in the AI + SE space.

  • Teams from day one

    Groups form on day one and stay together for the mini-project.

  • Demonstrations

    Product and technology showcases, plus mini-project demos.

  • Case studies

    Real industrial deployments and what broke in them.
